# Brag Plan: EcoCatcher (Baku focus)

## What is it?
An open, in-browser building-physics study of EcoCatcher: a traditional windcatcher (bâdgir / barjeel) rebuilt with six low-cost, recycled and solar-powered upgrades, tested city by city as a zero-grid substitute for air-conditioning.

## Who is it for, and what does it do for them?
Families, builders and planners in hot cities who cannot afford (or power) air-conditioning. It shows, hour by hour, whether one room stays comfortable without drawing electricity from the grid.

## What sets it apart?
A centuries-old tower brought up to modern standards: comfort judged against ASHRAE 55 adaptive comfort, sensor-driven louvers (ESP32 + SHT31), a 10 W solar panel for the fans, and 0 kWh from the grid. Every number is computed live by an open model.

## Angle (user direction)
Focus on Azerbaijan: Baku, where the author lives and works. Show the design meeting modern standards there, its advantages over the traditional barjeel, and what it offers a warming world.

## Hook (0–3.0s)
Kicker "Azerbaijan · 15:00", then giant serif "Baku. 31 °C outside." The project's own scenario, stated plainly.

## Highlights
1. **The model in use, in Baku.** The real interactive model on the Baku scenario. Start on "Traditional barjeel": room 27.9 °C, "Warm", 10 / 24 h comfortable. A cursor clicks "EcoCatcher (all upgrades)": pad, fans, chimney and louvers appear in the cross-section, the room drops to 23.8 °C, "Comfortable", 24 / 24 h.
2. **Modern standards.** While the cross-section plays through the day into night, three cards arrive one by one: "ASHRAE 55 adaptive comfort", "ESP32 + SHT31 sensor louvers", "10 W solar panel · 0 kWh from the grid".
3. **What it gives back.** Baku per year from the model: 750 kWh of AC energy saved, 338 kg CO₂ avoided, $289 to build. Then the real atlas table with Baku among the 24 hot-climate cities screened.

4. **Where it fits in modern life.** Homes ("One tower, one room… larger homes use several towers"), schools and community buildings (the Phase IV pilot is planned on the Absheron peninsula), and low-cost and informal housing (hand tools, salvaged parts, no grid). Beside it, the real roadmap with Phase IV marked.

## Punchline / outro (25.2–28.2s)
Seal "E" + "EcoCatcher". "No refrigerant. No compressor. No grid connection." (verbatim from the page). Credits: Maksud Yusuf · Jamal Naghizade · Baku, Azerbaijan.

## User flow worth showing
Pick Baku → switch Traditional barjeel to EcoCatcher → watch the day play out.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, editorial launch film in the report's own paper-and-crimson style
- Interpretation: few scenes, long readable holds, slow push-ins, soft dips through paper between scenes.

## Format: landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps
## Duration: 28.2 s (47 beats at 100 BPM)
Over the usual 15–25 s on purpose: the "where it fits" scene was added on request.

## Visual identity (from index.html)

## Sound
Original score, synthesized: warm D-major bed at 100 BPM (Dmaj7 → Bm7 → Gmaj7 → A), soft plucked arpeggio, light pulse entering at the reveal. Effects tuned to the same key and bathed in the same reverb: a soft tick on the cursor click, marimba notes on each card, a low swell into the outro, and a final bell on D. Effects sit under the music.
