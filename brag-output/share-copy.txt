A windcatcher from Baku, brought up to modern standards: on a 31 °C Baku afternoon, EcoCatcher keeps a 20 m² room comfortable 24 hours out of 24 (the traditional barjeel manages 10), with 0 kWh from the grid. One tower per room, for homes, schools and low-cost housing, with a pilot planned on the Absheron peninsula. By Maksud Yusuf and Jamal Naghizade.
